01 / About

Who I Am

I'm Adithya Vimukthi, a Mechatronics Engineer currently pursuing a Master's in Advanced Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at Queensland University of Technology (QUT), Brisbane.

My work sits at the intersection of robotics, computer vision, and intelligent control — from developing real-time motion tracking systems for human-robot interaction to designing embedded automation solutions for industrial environments. I have hands-on experience across the full stack: kinematics, control theory (LQR, MPC), ROS, Python/C++, and embedded hardware.

I have contributed to published research on teleoperation, worked as an engineering trainee in manufacturing automation, and built systems that bridge academic research with real-world constraints.
