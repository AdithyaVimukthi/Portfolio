About Me

Your Photo

I'm Adithya Vimukthi — a mechatronics engineer from Sri Lanka, now based in Australia, building robots that work in the real world.

My expertise spans the full robotics stack: mechanical design, electronics, embedded firmware, and control software. I don't just simulate robots — I design the chassis, fabricate the parts, lay out the PCBs, write the firmware, and tune the controllers until the machine does what it's supposed to.

My long-term vision: building robot-operated industries — fully automated ecosystems where intelligent machines handle production from end to end.
